# Salamander Grand Piano V3

The MP3 recordings in this directory are from Salamander Grand Piano V3 by
Alexander Holm and are licensed under [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

Original library: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3

The application selects the nearest recording and adjusts its playback rate for
the requested MIDI pitch.
