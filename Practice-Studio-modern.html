Before you record

Tips for a clear recording

  1. Headphones are not recommended for this practice session.
  2. Choose a quiet environment and silence nearby notifications or devices.
  3. Keep the practice-track volume low enough that it does not bleed into your microphone.
  4. Disable automatic noise suppression, echo cancellation, and automatic gain control when your device offers those settings.
  5. Place your microphone about 6–12 inches from your mouth and make a short test recording first.
